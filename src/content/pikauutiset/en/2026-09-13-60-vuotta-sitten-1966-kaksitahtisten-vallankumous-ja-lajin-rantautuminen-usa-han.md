- **The end of the four-stroke era:** Defending champion Jeff Smith (Great Britain) tried to defend his title on his four-stroke BSA bike, but the experimental bike's mechanical failures sealed the end of the heavy four-strokes' dominance for decades.
- **250cc class mud battle:** Sweden's Torsten Hallman returned to the championship ranks and captured his third world championship of his career on a Husqvarna after a tough, season-long battle against Belgium's Joël Robert.
- **Motocross landing in the United States:** Newly crowned champion Torsten Hallman traveled to the USA in the autumn to market the Husqvarna and won every one of the 23 heats he raced with complete dominance.
