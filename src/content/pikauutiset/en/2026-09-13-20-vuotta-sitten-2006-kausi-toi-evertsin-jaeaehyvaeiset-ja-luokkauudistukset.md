The year 2006 is remembered in motocross history for the end of the career of the sport's most successful rider of all time, the emergence of young super-talents, and the updating of class names to modern standards.

- **Stefan Everts' perfect swan song:** Belgian Stefan Everts rode to his tenth and final world championship in the MX1 class, winning an incredible 14 out of 15 rounds of the season before retiring.
- **"The GOAT" Carmichael and Villopoto (AMA):** Ricky Carmichael won the 450cc championship with the Suzuki team in his final full outdoor season, and young Ryan Villopoto took his first career championship in the new MX Lites class.
- **Tensions peaked at the Motocross des Nations (MXoN):** Stefan Everts won both of his motos against James Stewart at Matterley Basin, but a consistent Team USA took the overall victory to the United States, with Finland finishing tenth.
- **The revolution of class names:** The series officially moved away from class definitions based on engine displacement as four-strokes displaced two-strokes, leading to the introduction of MX1, MX2, and MX3 classes in the World Championship, and MX and MX Lites classes in America.
